Brand & Style

This design system is built upon a Corporate / Modern (Material 3) aesthetic, tailored specifically for an Android productivity scanner application. The emotional response is efficient, approachable, and delightful—transforming the mechanical chore of digitization into a warm, tactile experience.

Brand Personality
  • Precise: Laser-focused on accurate OCR and clean document management.
  • Approachable: Welcoming pink hues soften the utilitarian nature of document scanning.
  • Polished: High-end mobile ergonomics with fluid transitions and satisfying micro-interactions.
Colors

Typography

Utilizing Roboto Flex, the typography system provides extreme adaptability for variable font scaling, ensuring crisp legibility across dense scanned paragraphs and high-level dashboard summaries.

Scale Guidelines

  • Headlines use bolder weights (600) to anchor scanning modes and document titles.
  • Body text utilizes optimal tracking and generous line heights to minimize eye strain when reviewing transcribed notes.
  • Use body-sm exclusively for metadata such as scan timestamps and file sizes.
Layout & Spacing

The layout model relies on a responsive fluid mobile grid with standard 16px screen margins, optimized for single-handed thumb reach on Android devices.

  • Spacing is anchored to an 8px base grid, scaling upward via space-md (16px) and space-lg (24px) increments for content separation.
  • Bottom actions and Floating Action Buttons (FABs) maintain a strict 24px safe-area inset from system navigation bars.
Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through tonal layers and ambient shadows that elevate interactive surfaces above the pure white canvas.

  • Surface Tiers: Use the soft pink secondary color (#FCE4EC) at low opacities or tonal shifts to differentiate resting cards from the background.
  • Shadow Character: Shadows are extra-diffused, low-opacity, and tinted with subtle pink undertones (#D81B60 at 8-12% opacity) to unify the color narrative. Avoid harsh black drop shadows.
Shapes

The design system embraces a friendly, tactile Rounded shape language ( roundedleness: 2).

  • Standard Containers: UI elements feature generous radii (0.5rem to 1rem).
  • Cards & Sheets: Document preview cards and bottom sheets utilize pronounced curves (rounded-2xl to rounded-3xl / 1.5rem to 2rem) to emulate tactile paper cutouts ready for interaction.
